 ABOUT THE COLLECTION

Twenty years of everyday technology.

This is Harry’s personal history of phones, laptops and desktop PCs, beginning in 2006. The dates on the timeline are when each device joined the collection. Early dates are approximate where shown.

Five PC builds trace the journey from a Dino PC to the Meshroom D. The 2022 processor change is an upgrade to the Meshlicious build.

A note on the details

Recorded configurations come from Harry’s inventory. Product information, launch prices and published benchmarks are shown separately, with sources. Missing personal specifications remain unrecorded.

Device images are consistent, AI-generated reference illustrations, not photographs of Harry’s actual hardware. Unrecorded colours are illustrative. Prices are original-market launch prices, not purchase prices or current valuations.
