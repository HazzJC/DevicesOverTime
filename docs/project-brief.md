# Devices Over Time

The implemented viewer follows the user's supplied dark mountain-and-lake mockup: consistent device illustrations, blue illuminated platforms, timeline navigation and a grouped thumbnail collection.

## Authoritative inventory

Use [model-research.md](model-research.md) for all personal device facts. Its final confirmed records supersede the initial research candidates and original message spellings. Unknowns are intentional. There are 13 phones, 8 laptops and 5 desktop builds; the 2022 Ryzen 9 5900X is an upgrade to the 2021 build.

## Implemented behavior

- A chronological ownership timeline with mouse dragging, touch scrolling, arrows, keyboard controls and year overview.
- Category filters, searchable device names and specifications, thumbnail navigation, hover previews, and click/tap detail dialogs.
- Separate ownership and product-release information, sourced launch prices and published benchmark context where established.
- Responsive mobile layout and reduced-motion support.
- Static build for a dedicated Cloudflare Pages project and devices.harryjameschapman.com, separate from the portfolio.

Timeline milestones are evenly spaced for legibility, with extra spacing at year boundaries. The small year overview is proportional. Illustrations are disclosed as generated interpretations, not photographs of the user's actual hardware. No purchase prices, personal benchmarks or anecdotes are invented.
