## Data rules

- Do not change the listed model names, acquisition dates or confirmed configurations without a new user correction.
- Treat blank or “not recorded” fields as deliberately unknown; do not fill them from manufacturer configurations.
- Release dates, launch prices, public benchmarks and product imagery may be researched separately, but must remain distinct from this ownership inventory.
- Product photos are reference imagery unless explicitly supplied by the user; never present them as photographs of the user's hardware.
